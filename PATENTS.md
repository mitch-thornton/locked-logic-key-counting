# Patents

Some methods in this repository are covered by U.S. Provisional Patent
Application No. 64/145,457, assigned to Clearpoint Research, LLC.

The MIT license in `LICENSE` grants copyright permissions for the source code in
this repository. **It does not grant any patent rights.** That distinction is
deliberate, and matters in practice:

- A graduate student or academic **studying** the methods, reading the code, and
  reproducing the results for research or coursework is exercising the copyright
  grant and needs nothing further.
- A researcher **comparing** their own method against this one, or building on it
  in a non-commercial academic setting, is likewise covered by the copyright
  grant.
- A company **deploying** the methods in a commercial product or service may
  require a separate patent license, independent of the MIT copyright grant.
- Downstream open-source software that incorporates this code receives the MIT
  copyright permissions only, not patent rights; commercial deployment of a
  patented method would still require a separate patent license. Redistributors
  are asked to carry this notice forward so the copyright-versus-patent
  distinction stays visible.

For patent-licensing inquiries, contact Clearpoint Research, LLC.
